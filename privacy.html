Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Welcome to CVForge. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you use our website.

1. Information You Enter

CVForge allows you to enter information such as your name, email address, phone number, education, work experience, skills, and other information used to create a CV or cover letter.

The information you enter is used to generate the documents requested by you. CVForge does not require you to create an account to use the basic CV builder.

2. Google Analytics

CVForge uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, including information such as page views, traffic sources, device information, and general usage statistics.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect this information.

3. Cookies

CVForge may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics, website functionality, and improving the user experience.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and improve the CV builder.
  • Generate CV and cover letter documents.
  • Understand website usage and traffic.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.

5. Data Storage

The basic CV builder is designed to generate documents directly in the user's browser. Information entered into the CV builder is not intentionally stored on a CVForge server.

However, third-party services used by the website, such as analytics providers, may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Third-Party Services

CVForge may use third-party services such as Google Analytics and external libraries required for website functionality. These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

CVForge is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact CVForge through the contact information provided on the website.
